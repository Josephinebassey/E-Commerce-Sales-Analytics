
# E-Commerce Sales & Delivery Analytics
## Final Business Insights Report

### Executive Summary

This project analysed the Olist Brazilian E-Commerce dataset to understand merchandise sales, product performance, delivery efficiency, and customer satisfaction.

The analysis covered 96,478 delivered orders, representing R$13.22 million in merchandise value.

Python and SQL were used for data preparation and analysis, while Power BI was used to develop an interactive business dashboard.

### Key Business Findings

**1. Sales Performance**

Delivered-order merchandise value increased by 161.59% between January–July 2017 and the corresponding period in 2018.

This increase was mainly associated with higher order volume, while average order value remained relatively stable.

**2. Product Performance**

Health & Beauty generated the highest merchandise value at approximately R$1.23 million.

Bed, Bath & Table recorded the highest delivered item volume, demonstrating that the category with the most items sold does not necessarily generate the highest merchandise value.

**3. Delivery Performance**

The on-time delivery rate was 93.23% among 96,470 eligible delivered orders.

The average delivery duration was 12.56 days.

**4. Customer Satisfaction**

Late deliveries were associated with significantly lower customer review scores.

- On-time deliveries: Average review score of 4.29
- Late deliveries: Average review score of 2.27

Among reviewed deliveries, 62.36% of late orders received low ratings, compared with 9.23% of on-time orders.

These results show an association, not necessarily a causal relationship.

### Business Recommendations

1. Investigate delivery delays in regions with poor delivery reliability.
2. Monitor customer feedback alongside logistics performance.
3. Support high-performing product categories using both sales value and volume.
4. Track monthly order volume and average order value to understand merchandise sales growth.
5. Improve product category classification and data completeness.

### Limitations

The dataset does not contain sufficient cost, refund, or profit information to calculate net revenue or profitability.

Some historical periods have limited coverage, and the analysis is based on historical transactions rather than current business operations.

### Conclusion

The findings demonstrate how data analytics can identify operational challenges and support business decision-making.

Improving delivery reliability, monitoring customer satisfaction, and understanding product performance could help an e-commerce business strengthen its operations and customer experience.
